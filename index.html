ABOUT RECAPPED

Your music, your story.

Recapped is an independent open-source project that turns your Spotify listening data into personalized visual recaps.

Privacy

Recapped only requests the Spotify data needed to generate your recap. Your listening history isn't stored by Recapped.

Independent project

Recapped is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Spotify.

Open source

The source code is publicly available on GitHub. You can inspect how Recapped works for yourself.
