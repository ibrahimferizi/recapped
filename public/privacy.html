Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Recapped is an independent personal project by Ibrahim Ferizi. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Spotify. This page explains what happens to your data when you use recapped-music.vercel.app.

1. What Recapped does with your Spotify account

You log in on Spotify's own page, so Recapped never sees your Spotify password. Recapped asks for one permission, user-top-read, which lets it read your top artists and top tracks. It does not request your email address, playlists, or playback, and it cannot change anything in your account.

When you create a recap, Recapped fetches your top artists and tracks for the period you chose, builds the card in your browser, and uses your Spotify display name to greet you. Your listening data is not saved on my servers. The finished card exists on your device unless you download or share it.

2. What is stored

  • Login session. Your Spotify access and refresh tokens and their expiry, saved under a random session ID in a Redis database hosted by Upstash. They are deleted when you log out or after 30 days. The tokens stay on the server and are never sent to your browser.
  • Session cookie. One essential cookie, session_id, holds that random ID. It is not readable by scripts on the page and expires after 30 days.
  • Anonymous usage counts. When a recap finishes, a random recap ID, not linked to your account, is kept for up to 7 days so the same recap is not counted twice, and a daily total is increased by one.
  • Short-lived rate limits. To prevent abuse, counters are kept for about a minute, keyed by your IP address or session ID.
  • In your browser. Temporary data in session storage while you log in and while a recap finishes. It is cleared when you close the tab.

3. Services that receive data

  • Spotify handles login and provides your top artists and tracks. Album covers and an artist photo load directly from Spotify's image servers into your browser, like any image on a website.
  • Vercel hosts Recapped. Like any host, it keeps standard request logs, which can include IP addresses. Recapped also uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to measure overall visits and page speed; see Vercel's documentation for exactly what they collect.
  • Upstash stores the session and counter data described above.
  • Last.fm receives the names of your top artists from my server, so genre tags can be looked up. It does not receive your name or account details.
  • Pollinations generates the background artwork. My server sends it a text prompt containing up to five color codes taken from your album art and your most common genre. It does not receive your name or account details.

4. Example recaps on the home page

The recaps shown in the "From the community" section were shared by the people who made them, with their permission. If you appear there and want yours removed, email me and I will take it down.

5. Your choices

  • Log out in the app to delete your session from the database and remove the cookie.
  • Revoke access at any time from the Apps section of your Spotify account page.
  • Ask me by email about anything tied to you. Because sessions are not linked to a name or email, logging out and revoking access removes everything Recapped holds about you.

6. Security

Recapped is served over HTTPS, your Spotify tokens are kept only on the server, and requests to the server are checked and rate limited. Recapped keeps the amount of data it holds small.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
